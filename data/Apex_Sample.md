## 1. Patient Admission Policy
The admission process is designed to ensure a smooth transition of care while verifying patient identity, medical necessity, and financial clearance.

*   **Pre-Admission & Scheduling:** Elective admissions require pre-registration 48 hours in advance. Patients must provide insurance cards, photo ID, and a list of current medications.
*   **Emergency Admissions:** Patients arriving via the Emergency Department (ED) are triaged using the Emergency Severity Index (ESI). Life-threatening conditions bypass financial clearance and are stabilized immediately under the EMTALA guidelines.
*   **Patient Identification:** Two patient identifiers (Full Name and Date of Birth) must be verified via the wristband and verbal confirmation before any treatment or admission processing.
*   **Consent and Financial Clearance:** General consent for treatment is obtained upon admission. Financial counselors will verify insurance benefits and collect co-pays or deposits prior to non-emergency procedures.

## 2. Infection Prevention and Control
Apex Medical Center is committed to minimizing Healthcare-Associated Infections (HAIs) through strict adherence to evidence-based protocols.

*   **Hand Hygiene:** All staff, patients, and visitors must sanitize hands upon entering and exiting patient rooms. Alcohol-based hand rub dispensers are located at every point of care.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Staff must don appropriate PPE (gloves, gowns, masks, N95 respirators, or eye protection) based on the specific isolation precaution (Contact, Droplet, or Airborne) indicated on the patient's door.
*   **Environmental Cleaning:** High-touch surfaces (bed rails, doorknobs, light switches) are disinfected every 12 hours and immediately upon patient discharge using EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants.
*   **Surgical Site Infections (SSI):** Prophylactic antibiotics must be administered within 60 minutes prior to surgical incision and discontinued within 24 hours post-operation, per surgical care improvement protocols.

## 3. Medication Safety
Our medication management system utilizes a closed-loop barcode scanning process to ensure the "Five Rights" of medication administration.

*   **The Five Rights:** Staff must verify the Right Patient, Right Drug, Right Dose, Right Route, and Right Time before administration.
*   **Barcode Medication Administration (BCMA):** Nurses must scan their ID badge, the patient’s wristband, and the medication barcode. Overrides in the Automated Dispensing Cabinet (ADC) require dual-nurse verification.
*   **High-Alert Medications:** Drugs such as insulin, heparin, and concentrated electrolytes require independent double-checks by two licensed nurses prior to administration.
*   **Adverse Drug Events (ADE):** Any suspected adverse reaction or medication error must be reported immediately to the attending physician and logged in the hospital’s electronic safety reporting system within 2 hours.

## 4. Patient Discharge Process
A safe discharge process reduces readmission rates and ensures patients understand their post-hospital care requirements.

*   **Discharge Planning:** Initiated within 24 hours of admission. Case managers coordinate with patients, families, and external facilities (e.g., rehab, home health) to ensure a safe transition.
*   **Medication Reconciliation:** A pharmacist or nurse reviews the patient’s pre-admission medications against discharge prescriptions to resolve any discrepancies or duplications.
*   **Patient Education:** Patients receive a written Discharge Summary detailing their hospital course, new diagnoses, activity restrictions, and dietary guidelines. Teach-back methods are used to confirm patient understanding.
*   **Follow-Up Care:** Appointments with primary care or specialty physicians are scheduled prior to the patient leaving the hospital. Transportation assistance is provided if needed.

## 5. Data Privacy and Security (HIPAA Compliance)
Apex Medical Center strictly protects Protected Health Information (PHI) in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and HITECH regulations.

*   **Minimum Necessary Rule:** Staff may only access the electronic health records (EHR) of patients for whom they are directly providing care or performing administrative duties.
*   **Access Controls:** EHR access requires unique user IDs, strong passwords, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). Workstations must auto-lock after 2 minutes of inactivity.
*   **Patient Rights:** Patients have the right to request restrictions on who can receive their PHI, request amendments to their records, and obtain an accounting of disclosures.
*   **Breach Reporting:** Any suspected breach of unsecured PHI (e.g., lost laptop, misdirected fax, unauthorized EHR snooping) must be reported to the Privacy Officer immediately via the internal incident portal.

## 6. Healthcare FAQs (For Patients and Visitors)

**Q: What are the general visiting hours?**
A: General visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) visiting hours are restricted to 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM to allow for patient rest and clinical care.

**Q: How do I get a copy of my medical records?**
A: You can request medical records through the Patient Portal (MyApexHealth) or by filling out a Release of Information (ROI) form at the Health Information Management (HIM) desk. Requests are processed within 5-7 business days.

**Q: What should I do if I receive a bill I don't understand?**
A: Please contact our Patient Financial Services department at (555) 019-8372. We offer financial counseling, payment plans, and can help you apply for our charity care program if you meet the income eligibility requirements.

**Q: Is Wi-Fi available in the hospital?**
A: Yes. Free guest Wi-Fi is available throughout the facility. Connect to the network named "Apex_Guest". For security reasons, please do not conduct sensitive financial transactions on the public network.

**Q: What if I need to complain about my care?**
A: Patient satisfaction and safety are our top priorities. You can speak directly to the Charge Nurse or Unit Manager. Alternatively, you can contact the Patient Advocate office at (555) 019-8800 or submit a grievance via the feedback kiosks located in the main lobby.
